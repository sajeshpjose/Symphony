# Service flow (seven phases, three gates)

Tag: Skill = Claude skill today, App = must be built, Manual = stays by hand.

00 Scope: goals and KPIs (Manual); lead definition (Manual); services, pricing, contract (Manual).
01 Discover: research report v1 (Skill); client interview using the question list (Manual); research report v2 (Skill).
02 Set up: brand kit and logo (App); voice and tone with an approved sample (App); rules and claims (App); facts library (App); accounts and access (Manual); tracking audit and baseline (Manual). GATE: tracking verified and voice approved.
03 Strategy: audiences and pillars (Manual); channels and cadence (Manual); three-month calendar (App).
04 Monthly loop: five topics (Skill); drafts and lint (Skill); client approval, four posts per link (App); photos chosen by user and creatives rendered (Skill); schedule and publish with tracked links (App). GATE: first four posts approved.
05 Engage and measure: replies and review requests (Manual); AI visibility check, 10 to 20 questions a month (Manual); monthly report (App); repeat or stop (Manual).
06 Quarterly: strategy review (Manual); refresh old posts (Skill); run research again (Skill).

Added to the original list: goals and lead definition first; facts library; compliance and sign-off (claims, licences, consent, CASL, privacy; check with a lawyer); community management and reviews; content upkeep; AI visibility check; lead handling and invoicing; operations (pause switch, cost tracking, onboarding checklist, offboarding, permissions).

Build order for the app: 1 monthly loop (approval, publishing, tracked links); 2 reporting (monthly report, lead ledger); 3 onboarding (brand kit, voice, facts, checklist); 4 extras (Figma templates, AI visibility, upkeep).
