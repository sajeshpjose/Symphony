# MVP scope (agreed 29 Oct 2026)

The first release is the content engine only. Nothing goes to the client and nothing is published by the app. Figma: page "MVP".

## Flow
1. Connect the Claude API (key, models per task, monthly cap, usage).
2. Add a client in 5 steps: Business (with goal and social channels), Voice and tone (live sample), Rules and claims, Facts, Examples.
3. Run research (website plus optional GA4, Meta Ads and review uploads) and read the report with its claim review.
4. Get the next 5 blog topics. No client approval; untick any or ask for 5 more.
5. Claude writes each post overnight as a batch: blog (HTML and Markdown with FAQ markup), checked against voice, avoid words, claims, facts and length.
6. You review and edit, fix any failed check, then copy or download.
7. Social copy for LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram from each post.
8. Five image search keywords per post, with links to Unsplash and Pexels. You pick the photo.

## Not in the MVP
Client review links and gates, publishing and scheduling, tracked links, creatives rendering, lead ledger, monthly report, AI visibility, client interview and research v2, strategy and calendar, quarterly review, replies and reviews, offboarding.
