# Report sections

Version 1 sections, in order. Include a section only when it has enough verified content. The builder omits empty sections and numbers pages automatically. A thin site gives a short report; that is correct.

Fixed pages generated by the builder: the cover (first page) and "Sources and method" (last page). Do not write these.

| Key | Title | Needs | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `summary` | Executive summary | At least 3 included claims across other sections | Three to five plain statements on who the client is, what they offer, and where they operate. No recommendations that lack a verified basis. |
| `overview` | Business overview | Business name, what they do, where, how long (if stated) | Open with a `facts` strip (2 to 4 plain facts) when available, then one `lead` paragraph. Stated facts only. |
| `offerings` | Services and offerings | Services or products listed on the site | A table: offering, description from the site, where it appears. |
| `aspirations` | Stated aspirations and positioning | Mission, vision, values, or goals the client states | Present as "What the business says about itself". If nothing is stated, omit; ask in the meeting. |
| `audiences` | Audiences the business addresses | Audiences named on the site or evidenced by testimonials, case studies, or service pages | Segments only. No personas in version 1. |
| `customers` | What customers say | At least 10 reviews or clearly stated testimonial themes | Themes with counts only if sourced. Paraphrase. Omit if reviews are few. |
| `competitors` | Competitive landscape | At least 2 competitors with facts from their own sites | A comparison table of observable attributes. Cite each competitor's site. |
| `standout` | Where the business stands out today | Differences you can see by comparing observed facts | For example "The only business among those reviewed that lists X". Say "among those reviewed". |
| `opportunities` | Opportunities | A verified gap (competitors offer it, or this site is silent on a topic the audience needs) | Each opportunity cites the claims that create the gap. State the gap, then the opportunity. |
| `actions` | Action points | At least three included opportunities | A table of Action, Why now, Evidence. Each action follows from a verified gap, names what we would do, cites the claims behind it, and promises no outcome. Order by what we would do first. |
| `content` | Content starter themes | Verified offerings and audience needs | Five to eight themes tied to a verified offering or audience. No calendars or promises of results. |
| `market` | Market context | Statistics or trends with named source and date | Skip if you cannot source it. |
| `search` | Search demand | Search Console data (from GA4 overview or GSC exports) | Impressions and clicks, branded share, top non-brand queries, service pages that rank, blog versus service split, page-one-or-two opportunities. Numbers exactly as exported. |
| `analytics` | Website performance | GA4 exports, only when tracking is confirmed working | Channels, top pages, devices, key events by month. If the tracking sanity check fails, omit and put the finding in Opportunities. |
| `reviews` | Reputation | Google rating and review count for the client and qualifying competitors (places_search) | A comparison table of rating and count. Themes only from an export of 10+ reviews meeting the 30% rule. |
| `ads` | Paid media | Meta Ads, Google Ads or LinkedIn Ads exports | Spend, reach, impressions, frequency, results and cost per result by campaign and ad set; a bar chart of spend or results by ad set. Report platform-reported results and GA4 key events side by side when both exist; never reconcile them yourself. |
| `social` | Social presence | Social snapshot or LinkedIn/Meta exports | Per platform: followers, posting cadence, average interactions, engagement rate, days since last post; client and competitors side by side. |

## Version 2 additions

| Key | Title | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| `goals` | Goals for the next 12 months | Client-confirmed goals |
| `profiles` | Audience profiles | Client-confirmed or site-evidenced audience information |
| `positioning` | Positioning | Verified differences plus client-confirmed priorities |
| `plan` | First 90 days | Items that follow from included claims; no outcome guarantees |

## Charts

Use one chart where it makes a comparison clearer than a table: review counts across competitors, sessions and key events by channel, spend by ad set, monthly impressions. Keep tables for anything with more than one unit or more than two series. No chart without a caption naming the source and period.

## Layout guidance

- Sections flow one after another and avoid orphaned headings. Set `"page_break": true` only to force a long section onto a fresh page.
- Prefer tables for offerings and competitor comparisons, short paragraphs and bullets elsewhere.
- Use at most one callout per section.
- Target 8 to 14 pages for a full report. Fewer is fine for thin sites.
