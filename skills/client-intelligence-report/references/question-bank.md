# Question bank

Starting points for the internal meeting list. Write questions specific to this client; use these when a gap matches. Each final question needs `trigger` (what made you ask) and `site_says` (what the site currently shows).

## Goals and priorities
- What is the one outcome you want most from marketing in the next 12 months?
- Which offering would you like more of? Which would you like less of?
- What does a good month look like in leads, bookings, or sales?
- Is there a seasonal peak or slow period you want to even out?
- Trigger examples: equal weight on every service; no stated goals; seasonal services with no seasonal content.

## Customers and decision makers
- Describe your best customer from the last year. How did they find you?
- Who makes the buying decision, and who influences it?
- What do people ask before they contact you? What makes them hesitate?
- Which customers do you want fewer of?
- Trigger examples: audience not named; testimonials from one type of customer only; B2B and B2C mixed.

## Offerings and pricing
- Which offerings bring the most revenue and the best margin?
- How do you price, and how much of it do you want to show publicly?
- Is anything on the site out of date or no longer offered?
- Trigger examples: no pricing shown; unclear packages; services listed without detail.

## Competitors they lose to
- Which two or three businesses do you lose quotes or customers to?
- Which do you respect, and why?
- What do customers say when they chose someone else?
- Trigger examples: no competitors named; competitors found with overlapping offerings.

## Brand and voice
- How would your best customers describe you in three words?
- Which brands, in any industry, do you admire for how they communicate?
- Words or phrases you always use or never use?
- Posts or pages you are proud of? Any you dislike?
- Trigger examples: inconsistent tone between pages; no brand guide found.

## Marketing and measurement
- What marketing do you do today, and what has worked or not?
- How do you track where leads come from?
- What counts as a lead for you? How quickly do you follow up?
- Who has access to your website, analytics, social accounts, and ad accounts?
- Trigger examples: no tracking visible; contact form only; several social profiles with different activity.

## Constraints and topics to avoid
- Topics, claims, or competitors you cannot mention?
- Legal, regulatory, or brand rules we must follow?
- Approval process: who signs off and how fast?
- Budget range and timeline expectations?
- Trigger examples: regulated industry; pricing claims; health or safety claims.

## Priority guidance
Mark `must` when the answer changes the strategy or unlocks a report section (goals, decision makers, competitors, lead definition). Mark `nice` for detail that sharpens tone or content.
