Rake the leaves, mow a little shorter for the last cut, feed the grass once in late October, and keep watering until the ground freezes. Birch & Co. Landscaping does these jobs on every Toronto property each fall. Most homeowners can finish them in a weekend.

## When should I stop mowing in the fall?

Stop mowing when the grass stops growing. In Toronto that is usually late October or early November. Make the last cut a little shorter than usual, because short grass is less likely to mat under leaves and snow. Do not cut it to the soil. Bare patches let weeds in next spring.

## Do I really need to rake the leaves?

Yes. A thick layer of wet leaves blocks light and air, and it can leave dead patches by spring. Rake every week or two while the leaves fall. A thin scatter of leaves is fine. You can also mow over a thin layer and leave the small pieces on the grass.

## Should I feed my lawn in the fall?

Yes. One feeding in late October helps grass roots store energy for winter. Choose a fall lawn food and follow the label on the bag. Water it in lightly so it reaches the soil. Skip this step if the ground is already frozen.

## Is aeration worth doing before winter?

Aeration is worth it when the soil is hard or the lawn is thin. It makes small holes so air, water and food can reach the roots. Do it before the ground freezes. Heavy clay soil packs down easily, and clay is common around Toronto, so many lawns here benefit from it once a year.

## How long should I keep watering?

Keep watering until the ground freezes, but only when it has not rained. Water deeply about once a week. New sod or new seed needs more water than an older lawn. Before the first hard frost, turn off outdoor taps and drain your hoses.

## What should I do with my lawn tools?

Clean the mower, add fuel stabilizer or drain the tank, and sharpen the blade before you store it. A sharp blade is ready for spring. Keep rakes and spreaders somewhere dry.

## Quick answers

### Can I feed my lawn after the first frost?

Yes, if the ground is not frozen. Late feeding still reaches the roots. Skip it once the soil is hard.

### Is it bad to leave leaves on the lawn?

A thin layer of chopped leaves is fine. A thick, wet layer is not. It blocks light and can kill the grass underneath.

### When is it too late to seed a lawn?

Seed needs soil that is not yet cold. If frost has set in, wait for spring and seed then.
