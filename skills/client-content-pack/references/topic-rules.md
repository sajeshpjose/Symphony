# Topic rules

Generate exactly five topics. Each is a real question a customer asks before buying.

## Where topics come from
In order of preference: the client's service pages and FAQ, the questions in Search Console queries or "people also ask" results (if provided or found by search), questions raised in the client's meeting notes, and common seasonal needs for the client's industry and place. Skip any topic already covered by the client's existing blog posts if a list is provided.

## Mix
- Two early-stage questions ("What is...", "Do I need...", "When should I...").
- Two comparison or how-to questions ("How do I choose...", "How much time does... take").
- One ready-to-act question ("What happens when I hire...", "What should I ask before...").
Each must connect to a service the client sells and to a place or season where possible.

## For each topic give
1. **Question title** (under 65 characters).
2. **Who asks** (one short phrase).
3. **Gist of the answer** (25 words or fewer).
4. **Linked service** (from the profile).
5. **Image keywords** (primary and two alternates, per `creative-rules.md`).

## Rules
- No hype, no trend-chasing, no news-jacking.
- No claims, prices or numbers you cannot source.
- No two topics that answer the same question.
- Titles follow the voice rules: no em dashes, no banned words.
- Save the list to `topics.md` and show it in chat as a compact table.
