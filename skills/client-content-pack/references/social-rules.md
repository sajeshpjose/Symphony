# Social post rules

Only the gist. One idea per post. Written like a person talking, not like marketing.

## Limits (the lint script enforces these)
| Platform | Max characters | Hashtags | Emoji | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 200 | 0 or 1 | 0 or 1 | end with `{link}` |
| LinkedIn | 240 | 0 to 2 | 0 | end with `{link}` |
| Instagram | 140 before hashtags | 0 to 3 | 0 or 1 | no link; say "Link in bio." |

`{link}` is a placeholder. The application swaps in the blog URL with tracking tags. It does not count toward the limit.

## How to write
- Start with the point or a short question. No greeting, no "Excited to share".
- Give the gist of the blog answer in one or two sentences. Do not retell the post.
- Plain words, short sentences, active voice.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a full stop.
- No banned words (see `banned-words.txt`). No exclamation marks, or one at most on Instagram.
- No numbers or claims that are not in the blog post.
- Each platform gets its own wording. Do not paste the same text three times.
- Mention the business by name only if it reads naturally. The creative already carries the logo.

## Examples
Good (Facebook): "Wondering when to stop mowing? When the grass stops growing, usually late October in Toronto. Our short guide has the full list. {link}"
Good (LinkedIn): "Fall lawn care is simple. Rake leaves, aerate, feed once, and clear the hoses before frost. Here is what we do on every Toronto property. {link}"
Good (Instagram): "Your lawn is asleep. Help it sleep well. Link in bio. #lawncare #toronto"
Bad: "Unlock a vibrant, thriving lawn this winter with our comprehensive, game-changing fall care journey!"
